Getting started

After entering your mileage, add your first job on the “Schedule” tab — the “+” button at the bottom of the screen.

The “Typical jobs” screen opens: you can pick a ready-made job from the list — its name and intervals will be filled in automatically, and you only need to specify the mileage. Or tap “Custom” at the top of the list and fill in the form manually.

In the form, specify what to do and how often — by mileage, by time, or by both at once.

The jobs you create appear on the “Work schedule” tab — there you can see what to do and when.

How the next maintenance is calculated

Each job in the schedule stores the mileage at which the next maintenance is due (and, if a monthly interval is set, the month of the next maintenance). The app compares them with the current mileage and date and shows how much is left.

When you mark a job as done, the app moves the next mileage and month forward by the interval, starting from the mileage and month you actually entered. The old plan is not taken into account: if you did it earlier or later, the count starts from the actual values.

If both intervals are set, the app shows whichever comes first.

Job form fields

  • Name — what this job is: “Oil change”, “Brake pad replacement”.
  • Mileage for the next maintenance — the mileage at which the job should be performed. Required field.
  • Interval (km) — after how many kilometers to repeat.
  • Interval (months) — after how many months to repeat.
  • Next maintenance date — the month when the next maintenance is planned. Shown only if a monthly interval is set. Can be left empty — the date will then appear after the first completion.
  • Estimated cost — optional. Pre-filled in the completion form so you don't have to enter it again.
  • Note — for example, which oil to use.

At least one interval must be set.

If the entered mileage is less than the current one, the app shows a warning — but you can still save. This is useful if you are entering maintenance that has already been done.

Typical jobs

The “Typical jobs” screen lists popular jobs: engine oil change, transmission oil change, Haldex, brake fluid, filters, spark plugs and so on.

When you pick a job from the list, a form opens with the name and intervals already filled in. Check them, enter the mileage — and save.

The intervals in the typical jobs are approximate. If your car's service book specifies different values, adjust them in the form before saving.

What the card color means

  • Green — maintenance is not due yet.
  • Yellow — due soon.
  • Red — maintenance is overdue.
  • Gray — the job is postponed.

Postpone

The job is temporarily hidden in the “Postponed” section. The reminder is delayed for the selected period: 3 days, a week, a month, or a custom date.

The “Postpone” button is available on red and yellow cards. It is not available on green cards (maintenance not due yet) or on already postponed jobs.

When the postponement period ends, the card returns to the active list and looks as usual.

Complete a job

Tap “Complete” on the card. A form opens: specify the mileage and month of completion, and optionally the cost and a note.

If the entered mileage is greater than the current one, the app asks whether to update the car's mileage. If you agree, the current mileage is updated.

After saving, the job goes to history, and the next maintenance is moved forward by the interval from the entered values.

Complete several jobs at once

If you were at a service center and had several jobs done at once, you don't need to mark each one separately. On the “Work schedule” tab:

  • Long-press a card — this enables selection mode.
  • Check the checkboxes for all jobs that were completed.
  • Tap the “Complete (N)” button at the bottom of the screen.

A single shared form opens: specify the mileage and month of completion once — they will be applied to all selected jobs. Add a note if needed.

After saving, all selected jobs go to history, and their next maintenance is moved forward by the interval from the entered values. The car's mileage is updated once.

Cost is not entered in the shared form — if needed, you can specify it later in each history record separately.

You can exit selection mode with the “×” button in the header.

Why update the mileage

The app calculates maintenance deadlines based on the current mileage. Update the mileage once a month or with the frequency you specified in the settings.

If the mileage has not been updated for a long time, the app will send a notification — but only when the app is closed. When the app starts, no notification is shown: instead, an orange label “Updated N days ago” appears on the main screen.

History records

All completed jobs are stored here. Records can be edited and deleted.

History records do not affect the work schedule — the schedule changes only when you tap “Complete” on a job card.

Settings

  • Car name — displayed in the header.
  • Mileage reminders — enable/disable and choose frequency.
  • Warning thresholds — after how many km and months the card turns yellow.
  • Theme — light, dark, or system.
  • Export/import — data backup.

Export and import data

All data is stored only on your device. Use “Settings” → “Export data” to save a backup.

Import replaces all current data with the contents of the file. Before importing, make an export — just in case.
